Key Findings
Summary of principal drivers identified in this analysis
01 · AGE
Young adults (20–30) and elderly patients (80–90) showed the highest 30-day readmission rates, with rates above 12%.
02 · LENGTH OF STAY
Readmission risk rises with hospital stay duration, peaking at day 8 (14.24%), indicating clinical severity predicts both stay length and return risk.
03 · PRIOR UTILIZATION
Patients readmitted within 30 days averaged twice as many total prior visits compared to non-readmitted patients, a strong predictor signal.
04 · DIAGNOSES
Readmission rates climb steadily from 5.94% (1 diagnosis) to 17.65% (10 diagnoses), confirming clinical complexity as a major driver.
05 · DISCHARGE
Specific discharge dispositions (IDs 12, 15, 9) showed extremely high rates (42–67%), pointing to post-acute care gaps that need targeted intervention.
